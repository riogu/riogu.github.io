## Summary


This weekend I gave a
[talk](https://conf.gnu-tools-cauldron.org/prg26/talk/CNN8WR/) at the [GNU Cauldron
2026](https://gnu-tools-cauldron.org/), in Prague. 
Cauldron is where the GCC community among others (GDB,
binutils, etc) gather to talk about the state of their projects and share the work
that was done over the year, general work in compilers, and so on.

The talk was about my [GSoC'26
project](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2026/projects/iiWwmSYh) with
GCC's static analyzer, where I worked on improvements to C++ support. You can read
about the project in the [write-up](/posts/gsoc-2026/) I wrote.

Below is also the slides from the presentation and a link to a recording of the
talk.
